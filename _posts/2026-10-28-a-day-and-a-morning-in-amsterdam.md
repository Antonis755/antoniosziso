### A day and a morning in Amsterdam



### Preface
This blog post will be in two parts. Part 1 will be the story, information, tips and final thoughts on 
visiting Amsterdam, and Part 2 will be a not long text, about what the Amsterdam Dance Event is.
Each on his own, so if you want read it all go ahead, otherwise read the one you are interested most.




### Chapter 1:  Wrong train & Amsterdam
ADE was on my list to do/travel once in my life at least, and so I went for it this year. Knowing 
Amsterdam is generally expensive and not having enough time to spend there, I decided to do a 2-day 
trip.\
Everything started 2 weeks before my trip, where I requested two days off, and once I got the ok, I 
booked flight tickets and tickets for the event. Arriving in Amsterdam looked perfect at 9AM, but 
departing, there was no later option than 3PM.\
The night before I packed my backpack which inside included: DSLR (camera), more batteries for 
DSLR power bank, charger, chewing gums, fountain pen, a small notebook, wallet, ID, passport and a 
small shoulder bag for when I go to the club.

At 6AM EET (UTC+2) my flight departed and at 9AM CET (UTC+1) my flight landed in Amsterdam. 
I had already made plans about what to do, so I headed to the ticket machine and got a ticket to 
Amsterdam Central, but due to my stupidity, I managed to get to the wrong train which went to 
Rotterdam. Realizing this, I searched on Maps for “Rotterdam Central to Amsterdam Central” and 
hopped onto the next train once I was there.\
Being 1 hour and 30 minutes late and 1 hour away from my visit to Rijksmuseum, I took the risk of 
getting breakfast at a recommended brunch restaurant. It had quite a line once I got there, which made 
to wait for at least 15 minutes with the sun above rising slowly, and 15 to 20 minutes to order, wait for 
my food, eat and head to Rijksmuseum.

Moving around Amsterdam is very easy, whether this is on foot or public transport. From the center to 
the museum, I took the tram, which took an estimated 15 to 20 minutes and a couple of minutes of 
walk.\
Rijksmuseum was amazing, the Dutch have their own beautiful history of art! The museum offers a 
free guided tour through the mobile app, but you can also get a pad/touchpad for 6 euros, where you
plug in a pair of headphones or your headphones, pick a tour and listen to details about the paintings as 
you walk through.\
I paid the extra 6 euros using my iems and I took the “Best of the Rijksmuseum”.
The tour goes through the best Dutch artists, including the only and one Van Gogh, but I also wandered 
about other paintings and artifacts from 1700 to 2000. As I said before, I was totally amazed, and I was 
more amazed at how they portrayed Jesus, the ships models, including the one drawn by hand and 
pencil, but also The Holy Kinship by Geertgen tot Sint Jans and In the Month of July by Paul Garbiel.
Rijksmuseum is worth a second visit for sure.

After that I had a walk, lots of photos in the cloudy at the time city of Holland, and a worm flat white 
coffee in the corner of Oude Kerk Amsterdam.\
The next 2 hours ended resting and then walking again to eat dinner at a Vietnamese restaurant, away 
from center, a big hot Pho soup, which gave me the strength to arrive by 9PM the ADE event and dance 
for many hours.




### Tips and last thoughts:
-  Always read carefully the signs before doing anything, because you might end up in Rotterdam like 
me.
-  Use your credit card for public transport, it’s very easy to use. If you are from a country with different 
-  currency, I suggest doing exchange from before or finding a card with low foreign exchange.
-  HereWeGO maps is a very good tool to use, but also, you can use google as well.
-  Download maps and guides offline in case you don’t have internet access.
-  To wonder around sometimes without maps can be ok, as I have read many times, thatAmsterdam is a 
safe city, still be careful and alert.
-  Bikes are all over, but navigating without maps won’t be easy. It might seem fun, but you need to 
follow the crazy rules of the Dutch cyclist.
-  Taking public transport late at night? Check the schedule on GVB website first.

While I liked this graphical city of Holland, with its beautiful houses, canals and streets, I did find it 
crowded with tourists. The most important and interesting part is history and so the museums along 
with churches hidden between the many restaurants and shops. And to be fair, Athens is no different 
from Amsterdam.

### Chapter 2: Amsterdam Dance Event, otherwise, known as ADE for short

Now anyone that read chapter 2 tittle might think what’s that dance event all about?\
ADE is a festival happening for 30 years every October. DJ’s, producers, dancers and fans come from 
all over the world to dance mainly, but also attend workshops, conferences, interviews about electronic 
music. Record labels and ADE open pop-up booths selling mech or giving fans a chance to meet their 
favorite artists.\
Something worth mentioning is AMF. Do not confuse it with ADE, they are not the same, and they do 
not have the same quality. ADE for the ones who know is far beyond with a different experience.

Now that I have cleared this up, let me tell you that you need to buy a ticket for the specific events you 
want to attend. Otherwise, you need to get a Pro card, which gives you full access to everything 
including suit clubs, e-sim for 7 days and travel free for 5 days on GB (Public transport).

Apart from visiting Amsterdam as a historic city, my intent was to attend ITWT*.\
The event took place 25 minutes away from the Amsterdam Center, in a very quiet place, at the time,
that looked like an industrial area. I used once again public transport and walked to the venue.\
The venue looked like an old, abandoned aircraft parking space, with a big propeller rotating behind the DJ booth, lights were also behind the booth and above the dance floor.\
The event was perfect, full of energy as more people were coming by the hour.\
The DJ’s had amazing sets, including my favorite ones, Maria Healy, Craig Connelly and Sneijder.

In Trance We Trust: A Trance record label made by Tijs Verwest AKA Tiesto in the late 90’s, which since Tiesto left was taken over various artists. In the moment Richard Durand is in control.
