## The influence
After installing my satellite dish (another post that comes), I was going through the thousands of channels listed on my screen, when I found the only one legendary Euro sport. Since then, I have spent thousands of hours watching unique sports. But the one sport that made me watch and learn about it, was Snooker!
I was amazed, I have to say, well-dressed players, cues and balls on a way different pool table, from what I used to see in movies and in general (straight pool).
The first steps
After a couple of weeks, I went a bit crazy and I wanted to give it a try, but due to life and stuff going around, I never got the chance. Many months later, a friend of mine had a cue lying around, and I asked for it from him. He was more than happy to give it to me, since he does not play anymore, and it would be waste living it seat there dusted.

When I finally moved continents and got my cue back once again, I started to lay down questions, realizing that snooker is difficult to play, needs lots of practice, and a Snooker table is hard to find here in Athens. Only 2 places have them, and they are far away from where I live. And ofcourse something that any pool player would realize 1 paragraph back, is that my cue is not for Snooker, but for straight pool (also known as American here).
So, the closest and very easy to play was straight pool. Last Saturday I woke up, had breakfast and went walking to the closest billiards, which was approximately 20 minutes away.

## First encounter and changing tip
Straight shots 5 minutes after were easy to pot (to put a ball in a hole), but shots with angle were not easy at all, and same goes for the long ones. My aim was okish, but still needs a lot of improvement.  
After my first encounter, an online user told me that I should change my tip and buy some chalk. So, two days after, I went to a store selling billiards, bowling, darts and other similar stuff. I had a conversation regarding the tip, and I finally picked a soft Da Vinci 12mm tip, and a Taom chalk, which was the most expensive thing, at 19 euros, but I went with it because Judd Trump mentioned it once, and it stayed in my mind forever (it must be the name, Taom….).

The guy ended up showing me some snooker cues, which were amazing, but very expensive(200euros), and some snooker balls, because of the size difference.


## The goal and the feature
My final goal is to get better, put more balls and eventually play with others mainly, and as time goes on. Then I would like to try Snooker for sure! That’s what I came for. I would like to give it a try or two+. Who knows, I might end up being good at it.
I don’t know for how long I will keep playing, but it is something that interests me at the moment, and I want to play either alone, playing various shots or clearing the table, either against other players.

## Last words
My dad said, “Why do you get obsessed with billiards!?”, and I was like, “because I like it, and it’s a way to kill some time.” My dad will always be my dad, he is fine with it in the end. To be true, he also enjoys telling me about the time he and his friend sneaked in to play billiards, late at night.
