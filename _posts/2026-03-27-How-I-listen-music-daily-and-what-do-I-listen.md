A blog post about me and my every day music!

### How I listen music daily and what do I listen


The past!
Music for me was all my childhood and teenage years, meaning that, back then, we used to get a lot of music from the TV and Radio mainly, and later, through CD’s and cassettes. Shortly after, the Internet came, and we started downloading music from YouTube as mp3’s, from Daft Punk to Lady Gaga and going on. The next big thing were Itunes and Google Play Music, which introduced (for me at least) buying songs online and streaming services, and around there I remember finding Spotify (Around 2013-2015).
The world of music back then used to be powerful, all types of genres to listen to and discover, CDs and cassettes to exchange and buy, and as long as I remember, it was not as expensive as today.

#### The College and Today!

##### College!
As I was studying in college, my music taste changed. I used to listen to lots of everyday pop music, which later changed to electronic and its underground sounds (as people call it), and long thereafter some jazz here and there. My main way to listen to all these beautiful sounds was YouTube and Spotify, but once I found (Thrown in the garbage) a record player and a Sony DVD/Blu-ray player, I started collecting vinyls and CD’s. Then my very new friend, Giannis back then, gave me a home theater (found in a thrift store most likely), which hooked me up even more on buying vinyl and CD’s!

##### Today!
Today, I still continue to listen to YouTube, BandCamp, SoundCloud and, of course,  and CD collection. I stopped buying vinyl as much as I used to do, but I buy more CDs because they are smaller, they do not take up lots of space, and it has more music on it.
Since I moved continents, I only own a CD player named Sony Walkman D-EJ855, which does an amazing job on spinning my CDs day after day, whether it's portable (walking) or in my home office. I don’t feel like getting a record player at the momment, but for sure, yes, in the future.


My main genres today are:
Electronic: House, Progressive House, Trance.
Jazz
Pop
Greeks and Albanian folklore.
